All Projects

Engineering projects from NASA, APL, coursework, competitions, and student teams.

Space · Jun–Aug 2025

Flight Qualification of Derivatization Methods for the NASA Dragonfly Mass Spectrometer

NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. Prototyped a DraMS-like test bed for flight qualification experiments of DraMS derivatization capsules. Optimized a gas chromatograph (GC) program with tests of derivatized organic standards for calibration curve analysis, aiding identification of the chemically complex Titan surface.

Space · Jun–Aug 2025

Sample Path Leak Rates within NASA Dragonfly

NASA Goddard Space Flight Center. Developed a dynamic model of leak rates within the DraMS gas processing system (GPS) to inform sample integrity from integration on Earth to surface operations on Titan.

Rocketry · 2023–Present

Spaceport America Cup

Rocket Design Engineer, Aerostructures team, Rocketry at Virginia Tech. Manufactured and assembled the rocket body and fins to enhance aerodynamic stability and control during flight, optimizing for performance and cost efficiency. 18th place 10k COTS / 29th overall (2025 IREC); 43rd overall (2024).

Space · Jan–May 2024

Trajectory Planning for Alternate Europa Clipper Missions

Trajectory Modeling Lead. Developed contingency plans for NASA's Europa Clipper mission in case of delay, and for proactive planning of future missions to Europa or other outer solar system bodies. Optimized trajectories, comparing launch windows, time of flight, and total velocity requirements.

Thermal · Jun–Aug 2024

Combustion Grant: Metasurfaces

Johns Hopkins APL. Analyzed more than 26 metamaterial and substrate combinations to determine an optimal thermal response that guides heat away from sensitive components on hypersonic vehicles.

Additive Mfg · Jun–Aug 2024

Responsive Additive Manufacturing Structures for Controlling Thermal Events (ReACT)

Johns Hopkins APL. Revolutionized the thermal analysis process for a batch of novel 3D-printed thermal switch structures through automation in Ansys, decreasing personnel effort by more than 99%.

Space · Jun–Aug 2024

Thermal Analysis of a Subscale Rotor Stack for NASA Dragonfly

Johns Hopkins APL. Conducted thermal analysis and rapid prototyping on a scaled Dragonfly rotor stack to ensure thermal integrity and readiness for the subscale wind tunnel model preliminary design review.

Assistive Tech · Jun–Aug 2024

Enhancing Workplace Accessibility via Assistive Communication Technologies

Johns Hopkins APL. Prototyped a meeting transcription device to address the challenges of hearing-impaired individuals in classified workspaces, leading to its selection by the APL ADA President for further development.

Software · Jun–Aug 2023

Enhancing Software Robustness for Missile Performance Applications

Johns Hopkins APL. Improved documentation and constructed more than 25 class-based unit tests for analysis codes used significantly within the Missile & Reentry Performance group.

Design · Jun–Aug 2023

Design and Iterative Development of a Reentry Body Turnover Tool

Johns Hopkins APL. Engineered and refined 6 iterative design prototypes of a reentry body turnover tool for U.S. Navy applications, improving functionality, usability, and alignment with operational requirements.

Design · Jun–Aug 2023

Rapid-Response System to Reduce Emergency Times in Rural Areas

Johns Hopkins APL. Designed and iteratively refined a novel emergency response device, reducing response times for rural car crashes by over 41% and improving access to timely emergency care.

Additive Mfg · 2021–2023

ASME IAM3D Challenge

1st place U.S. & global (2023), 2nd U.S. / 4th global (2022), 4th U.S. / 12th global (2021).
